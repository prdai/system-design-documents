# why i thought of this idea

every project i've worked on has had the same problem- env management is a mess and everyone knows it and nobody fixes it because the bar to set up something "real" is too high.

the options are always either too heavy (vault, aws ssm, doppler for teams) or too manual (pass it around in slack and hope for the best). there's a middle ground that should exist and mostly doesn't- something lightweight enough to actually use for a side project, but solid enough to trust with real credentials.

the name senv- secure env- is pretty literal. the goal is just to make env management actually secure by default without making it painful.

what pushed me to actually think through a design for this was realizing there are two pretty different architectures worth comparing- a cloudflare-native approach that leans into workers + kv + access, and a docker-based approach that's more self-hosted but portable. both are interesting for different reasons and it felt worth thinking through both before picking one.

leaning docker right now- portability and control feel more important here than native cf integration- but that might change as i work through the details.
