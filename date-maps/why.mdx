# why i thought of this idea

a friend of mine is very tech savvy but is not the arts and crafts type at all. he wanted to give something meaningful to his girlfriend but the guy just can't learn how to make origami or anything like that. so this was a random idea i came up with and told him about- a way to use what he's actually good at (tech) to make something personal.

he was too lazy to actually build it though, so here we are- i'm designing it out and might end up making it for myself.

it doesn't have to be a couples thing either. friend groups, whoever- anyone who shares experiences and wants a way to look back at where they've been together.
