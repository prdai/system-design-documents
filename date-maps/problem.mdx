# the problem

there's no good way to visualize the places you've been with someone- dates, hangouts, whatever- on a map in a way that's private and meaningful.

people who aren't the arts and crafts type don't really have a tech-forward option for making something personal out of shared memories. photos exist but they don't capture the spatial story- where you went, the flow of the day, the route you took together.

and anything that does show location data (like google maps timeline) is individual, not shared, and not really designed to be something you'd look back on together.
