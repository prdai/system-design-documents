# why i thought of this idea

so me and a friend ([@Methika1234](https://github.com/Methika1234)) went to [keells](https://www.keellssuper.com) - a supermarket chain here in sri lanka- we grabbed a water bottle, went through self checkout, and got handed a receipt.

now here's the thing, sri lanka started this initiative to reduce polythene usage, so they began charging people for plastic bags. fair enough right- but then i looked around and saw a bunch of wasted receipts just lying there. like we're trying to save the environment on one end, but printing out all these receipts on the other?

some companies do send receipts through sms, but personally i hate that too- so i was like, wait, can't we actually automate this whole thing properly? we're a bunch of tech people, why can't we just build something that handles this?

and that's pretty much how the idea came to be- just a random thought at a supermarket checkout.
