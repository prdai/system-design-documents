# the problem

receipts are a bigger deal than people think- here are some numbers to put it into perspective.

## the paper and environmental angle

according to [green america](https://reports.greenamerica.org/skip-the-slip), receipt usage in the u.s. alone consumes around 3.68 million trees and 10 billion gallons of water every year. the production and disposal of these receipts emits the carbon equivalent of over 471,000 cars on the road. on top of that, 93% of paper receipts are coated with bpa or bps- and consumers report throwing away or losing 49% of the paper receipts they receive.

[wired](https://www.wired.com/story/receipt-recycling-uk-thermal-paper-digital-receipt) reported that the uk hands out about 11.2 billion receipts per year, costing at least £32 million just to make.

## so what's the actual problem?

all of this paper, all of this waste, and most of it ends up in the trash anyway. there's no standardized system for digital receipts- some places print them, some send sms, some do email, and most of the time you just lose track of them. it's fragmented, wasteful, and honestly just outdated.
